# Top 200 Landscaping Website Targets in the USA
Prepared: 2026-03-26

## What this file is
This is a practical benchmark packet for rebuilding or tightening a landscaping template.

It is built in two layers:

1. **Top 150 revenue leaders** pulled from the 2025 LM150 ranking by Landscape Management.
2. **50 additional website benchmarks** pulled from recent landscaping website roundup articles that explicitly surfaced real company sites.

## Important reality check
I did **not** fake a full crawl of every page of every site.
That would require a dedicated browser crawler and screenshot pipeline.

What this file does give you:
- a ranked target list
- verified official domains where I could confirm them in this pass
- a clean queue Manus can use for manual or automated teardown work

## Source stack used
- Landscape Management, **LM150 2025 rankings**
- Siteline, **Top 42 Landscape Contractors in 2025**
- Wix, **15 best landscaping website examples for inspiration**
- SiteBuilderReport, **41 Inspiring Landscaping Website Examples**

- Roast My Page, **Best Landscaping Websites in 2025**
- Freshy, **10 best landscaping websites of 2025**
- Jobber, **Lawn Care Website Design: 10 Examples to Inspire You**

Use Section A for scale leaders and competitive positioning.
Use Section B for design patterns, structure, visual hierarchy, CTA placement, service architecture, testimonial layout, and gallery strategy.
