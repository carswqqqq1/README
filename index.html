About Think Green

Family-Owned. Valley-Trusted. Award-Winning.

For over 25 years, Think Green Design | Build Landscape has been the Valley of the Sun's most trusted name in residential landscape design and construction.

We are a family-owned firm specializing in resort-style outdoor living and luxury curbside appeal. From the initial concept through final installation, our team of hand-picked designers, craftsmen, arborists, and horticulture experts bring every vision to life.

Whether you're envisioning a tranquil desert retreat, an entertainer's backyard paradise, or a dramatic front-entry transformation, we deliver quality that endures Arizona's climate and stands the test of time.

What We Do

Complete Landscape Design & Build Services

From concept to completion, we handle every aspect of your outdoor project with precision and artistry.

Landscape Design & Build

Custom design concepts with 3D rendering before a single stone is placed. We turn your vision into a detailed plan and build it to last.

Hardscaping

Custom patios, walkways, retaining walls, driveways, and decorative stonework that complement your home's architecture and Arizona's landscape.

Custom Water Features

Elegant fountains, cascading waterfalls, reflective ponds, and Baja shelves — crafted to create a seamless, tranquil atmosphere year-round.

Fire Features & Outdoor Kitchens

Custom fire pits, fireplaces, and fully equipped outdoor kitchens designed for the Arizona lifestyle — entertaining and relaxation in equal measure.

Outdoor Lighting Design

Architectural and landscape lighting systems that extend the beauty and usability of your outdoor space long after sunset.

Drought-Tolerant & Desert Landscaping

Water-wise desert landscapes featuring native plants, decomposed granite, artificial turf, and xeriscape solutions built for the Sonoran Desert.

Pergolas, Gazebos & Shade Structures

Custom-built pergolas, ramadas, and shade sails designed to create comfortable outdoor rooms and increase your home's livable square footage.

Irrigation Installation & Service

Smart drip and spray irrigation systems designed for efficiency and long-term plant health — optimized for Arizona's unique climate conditions.

Putting Greens & Sport Surfaces

Professional-grade artificial putting greens, bocce courts, and sport surfaces — beautifully integrated into your landscape design.

How We Work

A Seamless Experience from Vision to Reality

Our proven four-step process ensures clarity, communication, and exceptional results on every project.

01

Free Consultation

We meet at your property to listen, assess, and understand your vision — your lifestyle, priorities, and what "perfect" looks like to you. No cost, no obligation.

02

Design & 3D Rendering

Our designers create a custom landscape plan with full 3D rendering so you can see exactly how your project will look before construction begins.

03

Expert Construction

Our skilled craftsmen execute your project with precision, keeping you informed at every stage. We manage every trade — no subcontractor confusion.

04

Final Walkthrough & Warranty

We walk through every detail together, address any final touches, and provide comprehensive warranties on both our workmanship and materials.
